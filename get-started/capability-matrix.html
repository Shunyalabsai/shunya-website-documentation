Languages and voices

Supported voices and languages are confirmed per client plan and are intentionally not hard-coded in the integration guide; they may vary by product version or client plan. The guide's examples use the voice Meera for the standard and OpenAI-compatible endpoints, and nova for Pipecat.

Response formats

Select the response format required by your integration as supported by the API. Raw PCM output (response_format="pcm") is useful for streaming and voice-agent pipelines.

Latency expectations

Latency depends on the implementation (expected traffic, required voices and languages, output or streaming format). Confirm production requirements before rollout; the guide intentionally does not hard-code latency figures.
